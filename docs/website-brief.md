# Ready Margin experimental V1 — research-filled working brief

## Outcome

Create a complete, navigable frontend whose homepage tells a coherent restaurant story through native scrolling. The visitor should recognize a problem, understand what Ready Margin does today, see how the work helps and have a clear route to enquire. Ready Margin appears as a standalone new brand. Business facts marked below for verification are working assumptions for the prototype, not published guarantees.

## Proposed story

| Chapter | What the visitor learns | Image and transition | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | A busy restaurant can still leave its owner unsure how the business is doing. Working hook: “Your restaurant is busy. But is it working for you?” Pair it with a plain service descriptor. | Grounded restaurant after service, with a receipt in view. | Hook the owner's actual experience and immediately identify the service context. |
| Cash and cost | A full room does not by itself explain available cash or supplier cost. | Move into a receipt and invoice; show a concrete unresolved line item without implying a real client figure. | Connect a broad worry to a familiar object. |
| People and payroll | Shift records, tips and payroll can consume the time after service. | Travel through a shift/tip sheet into the owner's desk. | Make the second pain recognizable and show its connection to financial control. |
| Turning point | Ready Margin handles recurring finance work and helps the owner interpret it. | Let the scene settle; connect earlier objects to Run / Explain / Improve as a proposed method. | Show human-led work and the source of clarity. |
| How it works | Bookkeeping, payroll/tips, reporting and guidance are the working service pillars; exact boundaries require current confirmation. | Revisit a prior invoice or shift item as an explained action. | Demonstrate a service action instead of relying on an abstract dashboard. |
| Evidence and action | Show approved proof or a clearly labelled example, explain the first conversation and invite contact. | Return to a calmer restaurant scene. Main action: “Talk about your restaurant.” | Close the narrative loop and provide a usable next step. |

Use realistic restaurant environments with selective surreal object transitions. Treat the latest supplied Number 2 kit as the working identity. Maintain one coherent restaurant world across scenes and create mobile-specific compositions when crops fail. Keep text, controls and factual labels as HTML or accessible SVG rather than baking them into generated images. Scene imagery is illustrative, not documentary proof.

## Service and claim discipline

The supplied strategy research describes a restaurant-specific managed finance service. It also describes an internal payroll system, a developing dashboard and broader future product ideas. Present the current managed-service offer first. Describe software only at its verified release level. The newer brand book uses a broader operations/finance/advisory language; use that to frame customer problems, then make the actual service scope precise. Historical tier prices, client counts and quantified performance claims require fresh confirmation before publication.

Keep public pricing enquiry-based in the working design. Use the existing review enquiry path as the prototype conversion behavior. Do not imply a booked appointment, free audit, guaranteed turnaround or live software access without a working operation to support it. Testimonials, client logos, real locations, staff identity and security certifications need permission or proof.

## Application scope

The existing application already contains public routes, content JSON, optional CMS overlays, SEO metadata, consent behavior, a lead form and receiver integrations. Rebuild the visual frontend while retaining working behavior where appropriate. The homepage is immersive; supporting pages should use a coherent design system and preserve useful URLs until a redirect/content review is complete.

Native document scrolling remains available. Chapters must settle long enough to read, work in reverse, allow direct navigation and preserve a complete static narrative under reduced motion or media failure. Render the opening hook, service descriptor, navigation and enquiry route before loading heavy scene assets. No fake timed progress screen or global wheel interception.

## Production sequence

1. Write a scene-by-scene storyboard with headline, service fact, visual object, transition, reason and static/mobile state.
2. Generate a small contact sheet of coherent original scene frames, then produce approved variants and an asset manifest with prompt, source, dimensions, crop and intended use.
3. Implement and review one hook → pain → service slice on desktop/mobile and with reduced motion.
4. Complete the agreed homepage and supporting pages, then connect navigation and the real enquiry flow.
5. Verify build/type/lint, relevant existing tests, browser behavior, touch/keyboard, slow and failed media, form delivery, metadata and URL handling. Distinguish simulated checks from actual deployed behavior.

V1 is complete when a visitor can identify the customer, problem, current service, reason to trust it and next action without depending on motion. Verify the current operational facts and asset permissions before public release. The private discovery package contains 340 answered questions, their what/why and source status; its raw research files are not application assets.
