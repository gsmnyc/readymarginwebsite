# Ready Margin editorial direction

References: [Mercury](https://mercury.com/), [Linear](https://linear.app/), [Nory](https://www.nory.ai/).

Mercury informed the clear offer and dominant next step. Linear informed aligned workflow rows, task details and generous spacing. Nory informed the connection between staffing, purchasing and the financial decisions restaurant owners recognize. Competitor copy, imagery, customer logos and results are not used.

The homepage sequence is the owner's problem, responsibility and approval boundaries, a RUN / EXPLAIN / IMPROVE example, service coverage, inspectable working standards and the free first demo. The primary action remains **Book your free demo**, followed by a quote tailored to the agreed scope.

The website keeps Plus Jakarta Sans and the paper, ink, gold and stone tokens. Light and dark modes use the existing brand-derived hues. Workflow previews are aligned and action-focused. Books, Payroll and Food cost each expose sample task details with a finding, responsible person, next action and restaurant approval boundary.

All figures, statuses and prices in the previews are illustrative. They do not represent customer records or results. Providers are disconnected, and preview controls do not send approvals or change external records. The working-standard section describes scope, responsibilities and access; it does not invent testimonials, performance metrics or certifications.

The separate dashboard requires its source project before edits to Overview, Schedule, Attendance, Payroll and Weekly Close can be implemented. The public website preview is not the dashboard application.
