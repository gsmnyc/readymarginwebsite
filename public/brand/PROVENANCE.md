# Image provenance

Both photographs were generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. They are illustrative AI images, not photographs of actual staff, customers or manufactured goods. Native pixels are preserved; no upscaling is represented as new photographic detail. Exact vector artwork is supplied separately.

Editorial prompt: premium candid independent restaurant before service; owner and manager reviewing a clipboard, chef softly out of focus; charcoal workwear, canvas apron, warm cream/stainless/timber; natural light; no logos or text; negative space for the headline.

Merch prompt: use exact supplied Number 2 logo reference; photograph charcoal tee, apron, forest cap, canvas tote, notebook and cream mug in a coordinated restaurant/studio scene; natural light, realistic materials, no extra slogans or watermarks. Photographic logo rendering may vary optically; never trace it for production.
